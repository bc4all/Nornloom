# Your first duel in Nornloom

Every fight in Nornloom is a card game called **Bindrune**. This guide plays one whole fight with you,
one button at a time, with a picture of every step. It is the same fight as **Learn to play** on the
title screen (Hrafn's lesson), so you can open the game next to this page and do each step yourself.

**The golden rule:** in the lesson, the thing you should press next **glows gold**. If you are not
sure what to do, look for the gold frame.

---

## The short version

A fight is played in **turns**. You play, then the foe plays, then you again.

**On your turn, press the buttons in this order:**

| Step | Button | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Blót** | Put one card into the Well. The Well gives you Megin (your "money" for cards) every turn. Once a turn, and only if you want to. |
| 2 | **Play** | Tap a card you can pay for, then press Play. Warriors go onto the table. |
| 3 | **Raid** | Press Raid, tap the Warriors that should attack, then press **Raid with 1!** (or 2, or 3). |
| 4 | **End turn** | You are done. Now the foe plays. |

**On the foe's turn, you answer:**

| When... | Press | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the foe plays a card | **Let it be** | Lets the card work. (Or answer it first with a Galdr card, if you have one.) |
| the foe attacks you | tap the attacker, then your Warrior, then **Hold the line** | Your Warrior stands in the way and fights it. **Take the blows** lets them hit you instead. |

**Run...** leaves the fight. The game always tells you what running costs before you go.

**How to win:** every side has **Thread** (the number in the red shield). Hit the foe's Thread down
to 0 and you win. If yours reaches 0, you lose.

## The whole lesson, step by step

### 1. The table

![The table at the start of the lesson](images/tutorial-01-table.jpg)

This is the card table. The **foe** (a straw wolf, it is only practice) sits at the top. **You** sit at
the bottom, and your cards are in your hand along the bottom edge. The box in the middle is Hrafn,
your teacher. Read it, then press **Next**.

### 2. The wolf's Thread

![The wolf's Thread](images/tutorial-02-foe-thread.jpg)

The number in the **red shield** next to the wolf is its **Thread**. Think of it as the wolf's life
points. It has 5. Bring it to 0 and you win.

### 3. Your Thread

![Your Thread](images/tutorial-03-your-thread.jpg)

Your own Thread is in the red shield at the bottom left. When an enemy attack gets past your Warriors,
it comes off this number.

### 4. Megin

![Megin](images/tutorial-04-megin.jpg)

**Megin** is what you pay for cards with. How much you have is written under your name ("Megin 1").
You get new Megin at the start of every turn.

### 5. Your hand

![Your hand of cards](images/tutorial-05-hand.jpg)

These are your cards. The **number in the circle** at a card's top left corner is its price in Megin.
A card you cannot pay for yet looks dim.

### 6. The Shieldwall

![The Shieldwall](images/tutorial-06-wall.jpg)

The empty middle of the table is the **Shieldwall**. Warriors you play stand here. The wolf's Warriors
stand in the row above yours.

### 7. Your turn, in order

![Your turn, in order](images/tutorial-07-order.jpg)

This is the most important step, so here it is again: **Blót, Play, Raid, End turn.** Then the foe
plays, and you answer with **Let it be** or by **blocking**.

### 8. Blót: feed the Well

![Tap Thrall](images/tutorial-08-blot-card.jpg)

Tap the card that glows gold: **Thrall**. It opens big, so you can read it.

![Press Blót](images/tutorial-09-blot-button.jpg)

Now press **Blót**. The Thrall goes into the **Well**. You do not get the card back, but the Well pays
you 1 Megin right away and 1 more at the start of every turn. More cards in the Well means more Megin
later, so a Blót early is a good idea.

![The Well](images/tutorial-10-well.jpg)

The lit bead next to your name is your offering in the Well.

### 9. Play a Warrior

![Press Play on Karl](images/tutorial-11-play.jpg)

Tap **Karl**, then press **Play**. Karl costs 1 Megin. Every Warrior has three numbers at the bottom:

- **Might** (the sword): how hard it hits.
- **Armor** (the shield): it takes the hits first.
- **Vigor** (the heart): its life. At 0 the Warrior is gone.

![Karl is new](images/tutorial-12-unblooded.jpg)

Karl is on the table now, marked **new**. A new Warrior cannot attack on the turn it arrives. It has to
wait one turn. (It can still block.)

### 10. End your turn

![Press End turn](images/tutorial-13-end-turn.jpg)

You have nothing else you can pay for, so press **End turn**. Now it is the wolf's turn.

### 11. The wolf's turn

![The wolf plays a Warrior](images/tutorial-14-wolf-turn.jpg)

The wolf does the same things you did: a Blót, then it plays a Warrior (Fen-Wight). You just watch
and press **Next**.

### 12. A new turn: Dawn

![Dawn of round 2](images/tutorial-15-dawn.jpg)

Round 2. At the start of your turn (the **Dawn**) you draw one card and the Well pays your Megin. Karl
has waited his turn, so now he may attack.

Hrafn asks you to do a **Blót** again (with Skjaldborg) and to **play Huscarl**, the same way as before:
tap the card, then press the button.

### 13. Raid: attack!

![Press Raid](images/tutorial-16-raid-button.jpg)

Press **Raid**.

![Tap Karl](images/tutorial-17-raid-pick.jpg)

Tap **Karl** to send him in. (Huscarl is new, so he has to stay home this turn.)

![Press Raid with 1](images/tutorial-18-raid-confirm.jpg)

Press **Raid with 1!** to attack.

![Karl hits the wolf](images/tutorial-19-unblocked.jpg)

The wolf did not block, so Karl hits the wolf's Thread for his Might: 5 becomes 3. Then press **End
turn**.

### 14. The wolf attacks you: block!

![The wolf raids](images/tutorial-20-wolf-raids.jpg)

Now the wolf attacks with Fen-Wight. If nobody stops it, it hits your Thread.

![Tap Huscarl to block](images/tutorial-21-block-pick.jpg)

There is only one attacker, so the game has already picked it (Fen-Wight glows at the top). Tap
**Huscarl** to put him in its way. (When several Warriors attack, tap an attacker first, then the
Warrior of yours that should stop it.) The little tag under Huscarl tells you what the fight will cost
him before anything happens.

![Hold the line](images/tutorial-22-hold-line.jpg)

Press **Hold the line**.

![After the blows](images/tutorial-23-blows.jpg)

Both Warriors hit at the same time. Huscarl loses 1 Armor and 1 Vigor, but his Might of 3 beats the
Fen-Wight. **Lost Armor and Vigor do not come back** during the fight, so think before you block.
Beating a Warrior also gives you **Glory**, a bonus you can spend later.

### 15. Raid together

![Raid with both Warriors](images/tutorial-24-raid-together.jpg)

Round 3. Press **Raid**, tap **Karl**, tap **Huscarl**, then press **Raid with 2!** Together they hit
for 5, and the wolf has only 3 Thread left.

### 16. A chant window

![Tap Hold the Shield](images/tutorial-25-chant.jpg)

Before the wolf decides how to block, there is a short moment called a **chant window**. In it you
may play a fast spell card, a **Galdr**. Tap **Hold the Shield**, then press **Chant**.

![Tap Karl to protect him](images/tutorial-26-chant-target.jpg)

Now tap **Karl**. He gets +3 Armor, so he survives if the wolf blocks him.

### 17. You win

![The bout is won](images/tutorial-27-won.jpg)

The wolf blocks Karl, but Huscarl gets through and the wolf's Thread is cut. **You won!** Press
**Continue**.

## Tips for real fights

- **Blót early.** A card in the Well pays Megin every single turn. Cheap cards you do not need
  (like Thrall) are perfect for it.
- **Spend your Megin.** Megin you do not use is lost when your turn ends.
- **New Warriors wait one turn** before they can attack.
- **A Warrior that attacked cannot block** until your next turn. Keep one at home if the foe is strong.
- **Look at the tags.** When you raid or block, small tags on the cards tell you who would fall and how
  much Thread you would lose, before you decide.
- **Tap any card to read it.** The big view explains every word on the card.
- **Practice is free.** Hrafn the drill-master, south of Eikthorp's square, sets up practice fights
  against any beast. Nothing is won or lost there.
