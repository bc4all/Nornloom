# Credits

Nornloom is made by KB Info Krzysztof Bialowas. Everything in the game that is not ours is listed
here with its author, its source and its licence. The game's source and the raw art files live in a
private repository; this page is the public copy of the credits file kept there.

## Art: Time Fantasy by finalbossblues

All of the game's pixel art comes from the Time Fantasy packs by finalbossblues (Jason Perry),
https://finalbossblues.com and https://timefantasy.net, bought from the author's store. The licence
(from the store pages) allows use in commercial and non-commercial games, in any engine, with edits.
Credit is not required, and is given here gladly. Direct redistribution of the files is not allowed,
which is why the game's source repository, with the raw sheets in it, is private.

## The stories

Every main quest follows a myth as the Eddas tell it, and keeps its ending. The translations
followed are in the public domain: the Poetic Edda translated by Henry Adams Bellows (1923) and
Snorri Sturluson's Prose Edda translated by Arthur Gilchrist Brodeur (1916).

## App icons

## Word list (server only)

The world server's name filter uses the naughty-words list, CC BY 4.0, by Shutterstock: the npm
package https://www.npmjs.com/package/naughty-words, built from the LDNOOBW lists at
https://github.com/LDNOOBW/List-of-Dirty-Naughty-Obscene-and-Otherwise-Bad-Words. It runs on the
server and never reaches a player's device.
