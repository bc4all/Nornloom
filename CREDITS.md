# Credits

Nornloom is made by KB Info Krzysztof Bialowas. Everything in the game that is not ours is listed
here with its author, its source and its licence. The game's source and the raw art files live in a
private repository; this page is the public copy of the credits file kept there.

## Art: Time Fantasy by finalbossblues

All of the game's pixel art comes from the Time Fantasy packs by finalbossblues (Jason Perry),
https://finalbossblues.com and https://timefantasy.net, bought from the author's store. The licence
(from the store pages) allows use in commercial and non-commercial games, in any engine, with edits.
Credit is not required, and is given here gladly. Direct redistribution of the files is not allowed,
which is why the game's source repository, with the raw sheets in it, is private.

| In the game | Pack | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The overworld's tiles | Fantasy RPG Tileset Pack | https://finalbossblues.itch.io/fantasy-rpg-tileset-pack |
| The winter regions' tiles | Time Fantasy Winter Tiles | https://finalbossblues.itch.io/time-fantasy-winter |
| The people: the hero's looks, the villagers and everyone else who walks | Time Fantasy RPG Sprites 1 | https://finalbossblues.itch.io/tf-rpg-charactersprites-1 |
| The monsters on the map and the battlers on the saga cards | Time Fantasy RPG Sprites 2 (with Monsters) | https://finalbossblues.itch.io/time-fantasy-monsters |
| The item icons and the art of the forged cards | 1000+ Fantasy RPG Icons | https://finalbossblues.itch.io/icons |
| The portraits | Time Fantasy Faces | https://finalbossblues.itch.io/tf-faces |

The sprites are cropped, scaled and recoloured by the game's build scripts. The recolours are ours;
the drawings are the author's.

## The stories

Every main quest follows a myth as the Eddas tell it, and keeps its ending. The translations
followed are in the public domain: the Poetic Edda translated by Henry Adams Bellows (1923) and
Snorri Sturluson's Prose Edda translated by Arthur Gilchrist Brodeur (1916).

## App icons

The Nornloom mark, and every icon made from it (the app icon, the favicon, the store icon), is
original work for this project. No third party is involved.

https://github.com/LDNOOBW/naughty-words. It runs on the server and never reaches a player's device.
