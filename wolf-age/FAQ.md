# Questions players ask about Nornloom: Wolf Age

The same page as the FAQ in the game's help book (Help, then FAQ), kept here so it can be read and linked without opening the game. The numbers come from the game's own rules, so when a rule changes this page changes with the next release. A question that is not here fits a [blank issue](https://github.com/bc4all/Nornloom/issues/new).

## The story game

**Does Wolf Age cost anything?**

No. It is free to play, with no adverts and nothing to buy. Playing needs no account and no sign-in.

**Where is my hero saved?**

On the device you play on, in the browser's storage or the app's data, and nothing about it is sent anywhere until you sign in. Signed in, a copy is kept for your account, so the hero follows you to another device and comes back after that storage is cleared; the later of two devices' saves wins. The title screen keeps every hero on the device, so you can have several.

**Can I play with friends?**

Yes. Sign in, press Play together on the title screen, and make a hero for the worlds or take one of your solo heroes online; an account holds up to 6 online heroes and plays one at a time. Each world holds up to 100 heroes and is peaceful or PvP; in a PvP world other heroes may fight you anywhere outside a village. Together you hunt, trade at the market, and go as a party of two to four into the raid halls.

**How does the Lifeweaver mend others?**

Tap another hero to bind to them; a green ring marks them. Eir's Touch mends them, Shelter Rune shelters them, and 20% of the harm you deal to beasts mends them too, from up to about 8 tiles away. Tap open ground to let go. With nobody bound, Eir's Touch mends whoever near you is most hurt, and your blows land harder. You can mend only heroes at peace with you: in a PvP world, off a village's safe ground, only yourself.

**Can I change how a myth ends?**

No. Every main saga is a real myth from the Eddas and keeps its real ending: Thor loses to Elli, Baldr dies. You choose how the story gets there, and the retelling at the end asks what happened. Where the game invents something, the quest log says so.

**How long is the story?**

18 main sagas in one chain, from Odin on the tree at level 1 to Ragnarök at level 86, and 13 side sagas along the way, three of them in the strongholds of the high lands. The map climbs to level 99; after the story, the world reborn and the Norns' descent go on.

**Why can I not use my second art?**

The second art opens at level 4 and wants a point in your class's first tree. Until then only the first art's button shows.

**I fell. What did I lose?**

10 percent of the gold you carried, and one time in 10 a piece of the gear you wore. Nothing in your bag or your chest, and no experience. You wake in the nearest village you know, with 25 percent of your health.

**What do the words under an elite's name mean?**

They are its traits. An elite has one and a champion, such as a cave's lord or a land's old one, has two. Frenzied: under half its health it runs faster and bites harder, so finish it quickly. Shielded: blows at its front do less harm, so bait a swing, roll round it and strike its back. Splitting: when it falls, two lesser ones rise from it. Draining: every blow it lands mends it, so do not trade blows with it. Thorned: striking it from close by hurts the striker a little; arrows and spells from afar do not. Pack-caller: at half its health it calls two of its kind.

**What are named treasures?**

Gear with a name and one power that changes how you fight: arrows that fly on through a beast, a roll that ends in fire, a blocked blow thrown back. Each great foe of the sagas has its own, won in Skuld's retellings at the well; the harder the rank, the likelier it is. The lords of the caves and the old ones of the lands now and then leave others. A treasure is as strong as the gear of the level it was won at, and it cannot be sold or broken down.

**What are treasure maps?**

Now and then an elite, a champion or a strongbox leaves a map to a hoard buried in a land you can walk. Your plate says how far it is and which way, and the world map marks the place with a scroll. Stand on the spot and dig: what guards the hoard rises, and it is tougher than its kin. A dug hoard holds far more gold than a strongbox, always a piece of the best gear of its level, and one time in four a named treasure. You carry three maps at most.

**What is the world reborn?**

When the saga of Ragnarök is told, speak to Skuld at the well and choose The world reborn. Woken anew, the beasts of every land rise to your level, so every land is worth walking again and leaves the finds of your day; treasure maps then lead to any land. Two harder steps, in iron and in fire, make every beast tougher and harder of bite, and pay more gold, more experience and more finds. You can change the step, or put the world back as it was, whenever you stand at the well. The named foes of the sagas keep their level.

**What is the Norns' descent?**

A way down from Skuld's well that has no bottom, for heroes of level 99. Every floor is a deep cave carved afresh each day; its beasts are tougher and bite harder than those of the floor above, and pay more gold, experience and finds. Find the hole to go down. Leave a floor within four minutes and it is a swift one: each swift floor in a row adds a tenth to the pay of the floors after it, up to double, and a slow floor breaks the run. Every fifth floor keeps a gilded hoard. The ladder of any floor climbs back to the well and ends the descent; your record keeps the deepest floor you stood on.

**What is different in the high lands?**

From level 50 the lands grow stranger. Winged stones stand in them, and a great bare tree in the burnt lands and a white one in the far north, good marks to steer by. The beasts there know a second move: a draugr rushes, a wolf runs you down from far off, a troll puts its head down and charges, a bird stoops, a fire-thrower flares up when cornered and a spell-caster looses shots all round when you come close. And each high land has its terror: a champion with two traits that walks the whole land instead of keeping to a den, so it finds you as often as you find it. A fallen terror stays down ten minutes.

**What are the strongholds?**

Three walled halls stand in the high lands behind grey gate towers: Ókólnir, Brimir's hall, for heroes of about level 60; Náströnd, the hall on the Corpse-shore, about 83; and Surtr's forge, about 95. The world map marks each tower by name. Inside, someone at the foot of the ladder asks for your help. The first gate is barred until you throw the lever in the west wing, the second is locked until you take the key from the hall's keeper, and behind it the lord sits on a gilded hoard. Bringing the lord down wins a named treasure, and the lord can be met again in the retellings at the well. The east wing keeps two strongboxes.

**What do I do with gear I no longer wear?**

Sell it, or take it to a smith and choose Forge. Breaking a piece down gives scrap iron, and rune shards from gear with a quality, or dwarf embers from artifacts and set pieces. Spend them with a little gold to hone a piece up to +5, more of its attack, defence, health or mana each time, or to give a piece of tier 2 to 7 a new quality at random. Worn gear can be honed where it is; take a piece off before you break it down.

**I answered the retelling wrong. Is the saga lost?**

No. A wrong answer costs nothing; answer again. The reward waits for the right one.

**What should I do next?**

The HUD names the next thing to do and the Bag keeps the quest log. In the world, a ! over a villager is a quest to offer and a bright ? means they have something for you now. When nothing is offered, the beasts and the strongboxes around you are worth gold, XP and gear until the next rune stone opens.

**What is down in the caves?**

A mound with a dark door opens on a cave a few levels above its land. A hole in its deepest room drops to a second level, where the lord of the cave guards a gilded hoard. Under the caves of the high lands the way goes on, floor after floor, six levels harder each, down to level 99. The deep floors shift: each one is carved afresh every day, with new beasts, new strongboxes and a new warden, so the way down is never the same twice. The ladder by the way in always climbs back up.

**My bag is full. Where does my gear go?**

The bag holds 40 pieces of gear; potions take no room. Your chest at home, beside your door, holds 200 more and is safe when you fall: step up to it, or open the Bag near your home and pick the Chest tab. There the loot filter says which finds you keep; the rest are broken down where they drop into materials for the smith's forge. A find that meets a full bag is sold for what a trader would pay. Rare finds are always kept.

**A rune stone will not let me through.**

The banner at the border says the level you need. Level up on the beasts and quests where you are, wear the best gear you can afford and spend your skill points. The Map button on the world screen shows every land with the levels of its beasts, misted where you cannot go yet.

**The beasts are too strong. Is there an easier setting?**

There is no difficulty setting; the world is the setting. A name in green is an easy fight and yellow a fair one; leave the red ones for later. Rest before a hard fight, wear the best gear you can afford, spend your skill points, and watch a beast's wind-up: roll out of the mark and strike while it recovers.

**Can I play in my own language?**

The title screen offers seven tongues, and the game follows your device's language until you choose one. Some skill names still speak English.

**Does it work on a phone?**

Yes. In the phone's browser, hold the arrow pad in the bottom left corner to walk and use the round buttons in the bottom right to fight. An Android app of the same game is on its way.

**Will my hero survive an update?**

Usually. When the map changes, a hero from the older world wakes at home with everything it had. While the game is young a save may still be reset between versions, and the About page says so.

**How do I gather and brew?**

Walk up to a herb, an ore vein or a fishing spot in the wild and press Gather. Each kind of work is a craft that grows with use, and the far regions' richer finds need a practised hand. Herbs and fish brew healing and mana draughts on the Bag's Crafts tab: small ones at first, stronger ones as Brewing grows. Ore is smelted into iron at a smith's forge.

**Where do I report a bug or ask for something?**

On GitHub: the title screen and the About page have a button for it. Bugs, ideas and questions all go there, and they are read.

## Your account

**What do I need to sign in?**

Only an account with one of the sign-ins the title screen offers, and an age of 16 or more. Signing in is free and never needed to play. Signed in, your heroes follow you between devices.

**What is kept about me, and how do I delete it?**

The Privacy page of the game's help book lists it: your sign-in's id and the copies of your heroes. Delete my account on the title screen removes the copies at once; heroes on your device stay.
