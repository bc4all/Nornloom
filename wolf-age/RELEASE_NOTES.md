# What changed in Nornloom: Wolf Age

The newest changes come first. The game is played at https://fight.nornloom.com. An Android app is on its way.

## 19 November 2026

- **A training ground in Eikthorp**: by Hrafn the drill-master, south of the south lane, stand three straw dummies and a sparring post. Strike them as hard as you like to try your blows, skills, spells, dodge and shield: a dummy knocked down stands up again at once, and the sparring post swings back, slowly and plainly, and harms nobody. Nothing is won or lost there, no XP, gold or finds. It works the same in the shared worlds, the PvP world too, and the map marks it with a wooden shield.
- **An easier start**: the beasts of levels 1 to 3, the wolves and outlaws round Eikthorp and the slimes, bats and bandits of Ashgrove Meadows, bite softer, so a new hero of any class wins those first fights without drinking a draught. They still draw blood, and from level 4 on every beast is as strong as before. It holds in solo and in the shared worlds alike; bosses are unchanged.
- **Fixed**: a page of the game opened before the training ground came could show "Something went wrong" in the shared worlds near the new dummies. The worlds now tell an older copy of the game to update, and on the web the page reloads itself to the new one.
- **The Huntress has to fight for it now**: standing still and shooting was too safe, as beasts hardly ever reached her. Three changes make her hunt harder at the start and middle of the game, and fade by level 50, where beasts learn their own lunges:
- **Spear-throwers**: raiders, sellswords, skeletons, goblin chiefs and black knights now keep their distance and throw spears, about one beast in seven up to level 60. From level 50 they throw three at once. A hero who stands still gets hit.
- **Beasts rush the one who shot them**: a beast struck from afar comes at the shooter at a run for a few seconds, so draugr, wolves and bears reach a Huntress who only stands and shoots. Up to level 40 at full speed, fading out by level 50. It holds for every class, in solo and in the shared worlds; bosses and beasts that keep their distance do not rush.
- **A hunting knife for the Huntress**: she now wears a knife at her belt in the off hand, beside the two-handed bow. When a beast is at her side, her light blows stab it instead of shooting. An arrow loosed point blank lands at half strength, so let the knife do the close work, or step away. Smiths sell knives from the skinning knife to the godforged seax, and they drop from beasts too. A new Huntress starts with a skinning knife; a Huntress you already have can buy one from any smith.
- **Barbed Arrow takes Fox Step's place**: the Huntress's first art is now one hard barbed arrow at the beast, whose wound bleeds for 4 seconds. Fox Step's leap is gone, and so is the escape it gave; roll away instead. Your points in the skill tree stay where they were.
- **Fights make sounds for every class**: the Huntress's bow sings and her arrows thud home, blades ring, bolts of seidr crackle, and you hear a blow you take, a blow you block and a beast you fell, in solo and in the shared worlds. Before, a fight was silent but for the dodge roll.
- **Footsteps in the shared worlds**: your hero's steps sound as you walk there, as they do in solo.
- **Keys on the buttons when you play on a computer**: with a mouse or trackpad, or once you press a game key, every fight button, the arrow pad, the menu and Ride wear a small cap with their key: J to strike, 1 and 2 for your arts, 3 and 4 for your spells, K to roll, 5 and 6 for the draughts, WASD to walk, R to ride, and B, L, T and N for the Bag, Quests, Skills and Map. Phones and the Android app look as before.
- **Enter and Escape on every panel**: Enter presses the main button of whatever is open (open a strongbox, accept or hand in a quest, talk on, gather, fight) and Escape its Leave, Cancel or Back, in solo and in the shared worlds.

## 18 November 2026

- **Fox Step works in the shared worlds**: a leap that crossed the edge of the ground your phone keeps round you flew the Huntress off across the map and pulled her back a moment later, which shook the screen, left part of it dark and froze the game for a second. She now comes down where she leapt to, walking or standing.
- **Your target stays your target**: tap a beast and your blows, shots and charged shots go at it even while you walk away, so a Huntress, a Seidr-worker or a Lifeweaver can back off and keep shooting it. Before, walking far enough in a shared world quietly dropped the target, and shots flew the way you walked. A leap such as Fox Step still goes the way you push.
- **No more shaking screen when you use a skill while walking**: on a phone, a thumb on a skill button while the other held the arrows made the browser zoom and slide the whole page, so the screen shook, part of it went dark and the skill sometimes did not fire. The game screen no longer lets two fingers zoom it, in solo and online.
- **Every land has its own work now**: the lands no longer all ask to cull four beasts and open one strongbox. Each land has a board of three jobs drawn from many kinds: hunts of up to a dozen of a beast, clearing up to sixteen of anything that walks the land, its elites, several strongboxes, a word carried to the elder next door, scouting the land beside, a riddle from the old stories, the lord of the land's cave or a high land's terror. In a hamlet the healer, the smith or the trader may ask for herbs, ore or fish, from any patch, vein or shore. The jobs work the same in the shared worlds.
- Lands you have already worked through offer their new jobs too. A cull or a strongbox job you had under way can still be finished and handed in.
- **Something for every class at the smiths**: every class now has its own charm, worn in the amulet slot: a bear-claw for the Berserker, a shield-ring for the Shieldmaiden, an arrow charm for the Huntress, a spirit bead for the Seidr-worker and a healing knot for the Lifeweaver, from carved ones at the start to godforged ones at the end. Every smith sells each class a weapon and a charm of its level. Sigrun in Eikthorp now sells bows, staffs and wands too, and Leif's lodge sells wands.
- **Finds for every class alike**: beasts, bosses, strongboxes and dug hoards now drop gear every class can use in about the same share, about three finds in five for each. Before, the melee classes' blades and shields made up most finds.
- **Walking in the shared worlds is smooth on every phone**: a phone that stutters for a moment no longer pulls your hero back or leaves it walking in place.
- **Zoom out as far as in solo** in the shared worlds, holding your phone upright or on its side.
- **Let go of the hero you are playing**: on the Play together screen the hero you play now has its own "Let go" under its name, as the others do. Before, only your other heroes could be let go, so a single online hero could never be.
- **Back where you left**: leave a shared world and come back, and your hero stands where you left it (left in a cave, at the cave's mouth).
- **No more nameless "Hero" in the shared worlds**: the halves of a beast that splits, a pack a beast calls in and a boss's helpers now look and are named as in solo. Before, they showed as a hero called "Hero", jumped about, and could not be tapped.
- **Skills no longer shake the screen in the shared worlds**: on a slow phone or a slow line, a Fox Step, a Shield Rush or any art that carries your hero kept snapping it back and forth. Your hero now leaps, rolls and strikes as smoothly as in solo.
- **A fall in the shared worlds wakes you in the nearest village you know**, or at home if that is nearer, as in solo. Since coming back where you left, a fallen hero woke out in the wild instead.
- **A quicker wake after a fall online**: two seconds instead of five, or five while a friend stands beside you to lift you, and the world now says where you woke, as in solo.
- **The ground is drawn further ahead of your hero**, so a quick leap on a slow phone no longer shows black ground for a moment.
- **Beasts leave you be while you talk to someone in the shared worlds**, as the solo world stands still while you talk. It holds for a minute and a half at most (closing and opening the talk does not add time) and ends at your first blow, roll or skill or when you walk away. Other heroes in a PvP world can still strike you.
- **Close the chat in the shared worlds**: tap the x beside the chat to close it, and the game remembers it next time. The whole chat goes, your own lines too. A small Chat button stands where it was and brings it back, with a gold dot while someone is talking, and Say opens it again too. (The first try put a Hide chat button in the menu, but it left your own lines on screen.)
- **Calm ground round the villagers**: no beast makes its home within seven steps of anyone you can talk to, so the Wanderer, Bjorn and everyone else who gives quests no longer stand among slimes and bats. Beasts that lived there have moved a little way off.
- **What you gather is in the Bag**: herbs, ore, fish and the forge's scrap iron, rune shards and dwarf embers now sit in the Bag beside your gear, on the All tab and on a new Goods tab. Tap one to see where it is found, what it is brewed or smelted into and what it fetches.
- **Sell what you gather**: every trader buys your goods, one at a time or all at once, in solo and in the shared worlds. Online you can also offer them on the market, to heroes of your own kind of world.
- **The crafts are trees now**: Herb lore, Mining, Fishing, Brewing and the knots each have a branching tree on the Bag's Crafts tab, laid out like the class skill trees. In a gathering craft the finds run down the middle, the smith's tools branch off to one side and the craft's steps to the other (Quick hands, Bountiful, Full nets, Smelter, Rune seam), meeting in a master's step at level 10. Brewing branches into healing and mana draughts, with Steady hand and the master brewer between; tap a draught to brew it. Gold is what you have, green with a + what you can have now, dimmed with a number what waits for a craft level.
- **Boon draughts and a new craft, Cooking**: Brewing now also makes the draught of fury (critical hits more often), the shield-wall draught (blocks more often) and the fox draught (skills ready sooner), each for ten minutes. Cooking, a fifth craft with its own tree, turns fish and herbs into herring broth, eel stew and an ice char feast, which strengthen you for twenty minutes. One draught and one meal can work at once; the Bag shows how long they have left.
- **Tools and knots from the smith**: every smith now makes gathering tools from your goods, a sickle, a pick and a net in three grades each, and three gathering knots worn in the amulet slot. They bring a second find more often and more craft XP at every pick, in solo and in the shared worlds. Look under Smith's work at any forge.

## 16 November 2026

- **A new class, the Lifeweaver**: a healer with a wand and runes of life. Its blows are the lightest of all, but it keeps others standing. Tap another hero to bind to them: Eir's Touch mends them, the Shelter Rune makes blows on them land lighter, and part of every blow you land mends them too. Alone, a Lifeweaver strikes harder and mends itself, so it plays solo as well. It has its own wands, two skill trees and three armour sets. In a PvP world, outside a village, it can mend only itself.
- **The shared worlds play like solo**: the same screen, name tags over every hero, the Bag and the Skills, zoom in and out, smoother walking.
- **Quests online**: villagers stand in the shared worlds and give, follow and reward quests and riddles; shops, the smith, the healer, waystones, stables and temples all work there.
- **Strongboxes, caves and treasure maps online**: open a strongbox and fight its guardian, walk through cave mouths and stronghold gates into caves shared with your world, and dig up the hoards your maps lead to.
- **Ride online**: Ride and Walk work in the shared worlds, and other heroes see you in the saddle.
- **The market is kept by the traders**: talk to Astrid, Orm or a hamlet's merchant in a shared world to buy and sell; the Play together screen no longer has a Market button.
- **Several online heroes**: an account can now keep up to six heroes for the shared worlds. Make a new one for the worlds whenever you like, or take a solo hero online, and pick which one to play on the Play together screen. A hero you no longer want can be let go to make room.

## 15 November 2026

- **A horse is now a mark of wealth**: it costs 100000 gold, not 250, and only the hamlets of the lands from level 30 keep a stable. Eikthorp has none any more. Horses bought before this change have gone back to the stable.
- **The handbook**: the rules in full, printed from the game's own numbers. Every class and its arts, every skill of every tree, the spells, the gods, the armour sets, every land with its beasts, the strongholds, the raid halls and what comes after the story. Help links it from every page.
- **First steps in pictures**: a guide for new players, from making a hero to the first fight, the bag, the skills, the map and a fall.
- **Help knows the shared worlds are open** and links the guides, the FAQ and the walkthrough.
- **Fixed**: Help said every class has armour sets at three levels only; it now names the first and the last, and the handbook lists them all.
- **Fixed**: "Roll a name" no longer spills off a narrow phone screen.

## 14 November 2026

- **New skill trees**: three trees for every class, drawn as trees. Two belong to your class, and the Nine Worlds, with fire, storm and frost spells, is shared by everyone. A reset gives every point back.
- **Eight gods**: pledge yourself to Odin, Thor, Týr, Freyja, Njörðr, Skaði, Loki or Hel for two blessings that grow with your level and a gift of their own.
- **Fixed**: the game no longer freezes while walking when two beasts' name tags meet.

## 13 November 2026

- **Shared worlds are open**: sign in and press Play together. A peaceful world, and a PvP world where heroes may fight anywhere outside a village.
- A level gained fills your mana as well as your health.

## 12 November 2026

- **Play together**: an online hero, a chat for those nearby, mute and report.
- **The market**: offer gear and buy what others offer.
- **PvP**: in a PvP world a beaten hero drops some of the gold carried.
- **Raid halls**: four halls for a party of two to four heroes, each with a lord too strong for one.
- **Armour sets for every class**, early, mid and late, and the bag shows how far each set is complete.
- **Off hand and two-handed weapons**: a Berserker may hold a second weapon; bows, staffs and great axes take both hands; each class has its weapons.
- **Help rewritten**, with pages on fighting and on growing your hero, in every language.
- **Fixed**: a hero saved on the device is listed again, and two open copies of the game keep one list.

## 11 November 2026

- **The game has its name**: Nornloom: Wolf Age, with its own mark.
- **A home at any village**: make a village home at its waystone. A fall wakes you in the nearest village you know.
- **A longer climb**: level 99 takes about 100 hours of play with quests. Heroes keep the level they have.
- Resting is quicker to start, mana can run dry, beasts walk round each other, and the skill buttons are clearer.
- **Fixed**: beasts left alone no longer turn on the spot at the edge of their ground.

## 10 November 2026

- **Play offline**: the site keeps the whole game on your device.
- **The walkthrough** at fight.nornloom.com/guide: every saga step by step.
- **Three strongholds** in the high lands: Okolnir, Nastrond and Surtr's Forge.
- **Five side sagas** for the high lands, landmarks, and a roaming terror in every land from level 50.
- **The Norns' descent**: an endless way down from Skuld's well for heroes of level 99.
- **The world reborn**: after Ragnarök the beasts of every land can rise to your level, at three steps.
- **Treasure maps** to buried hoards, and **26 named treasures**, each with a power that changes how a class plays.
- **Retellings**: fight any finished saga's great fight again at the well.
- **Renown**: past level 99 the experience bar keeps filling, and each fill buys a small gift.
- **Beast traits**: elites carry one special power and champions two.
- **Cloud saves**: signed in, your heroes are kept for your account.
- **Gathering and brewing**: herbs, ore and fish, draughts to brew, ore to smelt.
- **Fast travel** between waystones you have touched, and **a horse** for the long roads.
- **A forge at every smith**: break gear down, hone it to +5, give it a new quality.
- **A chest at home** and a loot filter that breaks unwanted finds down for the forge.
- **A dodge roll**, a loot feed, deeper caves, and set-piece bosses for the main story.
- Arts cost mana, and the skill buttons show the cost.
- **Fixed**: resting at the inn heals again; changing gear keeps what is cooling down; closing the bag or the skills no longer swings a blow; a skill pressed before it is ready strikes instead.

## 9 November 2026

- **The open world**: Nornloom's world, from the meadows of Eikthorp to level 99, with every fight in real time on the map itself.
- **Four classes**, Berserker, Shieldmaiden, Huntress and Seidr-worker, each with its own arts and skill tree.
- Beasts that come for you or leave you be, elites and champions, finds common to rare, gear with critical hits, blocks and mana, bounties and a bestiary.
- The site's own address: fight.nornloom.com.
