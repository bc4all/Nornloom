# What changed in Nornloom: Wolf Age

The newest changes come first. The game is played at https://fight.nornloom.com. An Android app is on its way.

## 15 November 2026

- **The handbook**: the rules in full, printed from the game's own numbers. Every class and its arts, every skill of every tree, the spells, the gods, the armour sets, every land with its beasts, the strongholds, the raid halls and what comes after the story. Help links it from every page.
- **First steps in pictures**: a guide for new players, from making a hero to the first fight, the bag, the skills, the map and a fall.
- **Help knows the shared worlds are open** and links the guides, the FAQ and the walkthrough.
- **Fixed**: Help said every class has armour sets at three levels only; it now names the first and the last, and the handbook lists them all.
- **Fixed**: "Roll a name" no longer spills off a narrow phone screen.

## 14 November 2026

- **New skill trees**: three trees for every class, drawn as trees. Two belong to your class, and the Nine Worlds, with fire, storm and frost spells, is shared by everyone. A reset gives every point back.
- **Eight gods**: pledge yourself to Odin, Thor, Týr, Freyja, Njörðr, Skaði, Loki or Hel for two blessings that grow with your level and a gift of their own.
- **Fixed**: the game no longer freezes while walking when two beasts' name tags meet.

## 13 November 2026

- **Shared worlds are open**: sign in and press Play together. A peaceful world, and a PvP world where heroes may fight anywhere outside a village.
- A level gained fills your mana as well as your health.

## 12 November 2026

- **Play together**: an online hero, a chat for those nearby, mute and report.
- **The market**: offer gear and buy what others offer.
- **PvP**: in a PvP world a beaten hero drops some of the gold carried.
- **Raid halls**: four halls for a party of two to four heroes, each with a lord too strong for one.
- **Armour sets for every class**, early, mid and late, and the bag shows how far each set is complete.
- **Off hand and two-handed weapons**: a Berserker may hold a second weapon; bows, staffs and great axes take both hands; each class has its weapons.
- **Help rewritten**, with pages on fighting and on growing your hero, in every language.
- **Fixed**: a hero saved on the device is listed again, and two open copies of the game keep one list.

## 11 November 2026

- **The game has its name**: Nornloom: Wolf Age, with its own mark.
- **A home at any village**: make a village home at its waystone. A fall wakes you in the nearest village you know.
- **A longer climb**: level 99 takes about 100 hours of play with quests. Heroes keep the level they have.
- Resting is quicker to start, mana can run dry, beasts walk round each other, and the skill buttons are clearer.
- **Fixed**: beasts left alone no longer turn on the spot at the edge of their ground.

## 10 November 2026

- **Play offline**: the site keeps the whole game on your device.
- **The walkthrough** at fight.nornloom.com/guide: every saga step by step.
- **Three strongholds** in the high lands: Okolnir, Nastrond and Surtr's Forge.
- **Five side sagas** for the high lands, landmarks, and a roaming terror in every land from level 50.
- **The Norns' descent**: an endless way down from Skuld's well for heroes of level 99.
- **The world reborn**: after Ragnarök the beasts of every land can rise to your level, at three steps.
- **Treasure maps** to buried hoards, and **26 named treasures**, each with a power that changes how a class plays.
- **Retellings**: fight any finished saga's great fight again at the well.
- **Renown**: past level 99 the experience bar keeps filling, and each fill buys a small gift.
- **Beast traits**: elites carry one special power and champions two.
- **Cloud saves**: signed in, your heroes are kept for your account.
- **Gathering and brewing**: herbs, ore and fish, draughts to brew, ore to smelt.
- **Fast travel** between waystones you have touched, and **a horse** for the long roads.
- **A forge at every smith**: break gear down, hone it to +5, give it a new quality.
- **A chest at home** and a loot filter that breaks unwanted finds down for the forge.
- **A dodge roll**, a loot feed, deeper caves, and set-piece bosses for the main story.
- Arts cost mana, and the skill buttons show the cost.
- **Fixed**: resting at the inn heals again; changing gear keeps what is cooling down; closing the bag or the skills no longer swings a blow; a skill pressed before it is ready strikes instead.

## 9 November 2026

- **The open world**: Nornloom's world, from the meadows of Eikthorp to level 99, with every fight in real time on the map itself.
- **Four classes**, Berserker, Shieldmaiden, Huntress and Seidr-worker, each with its own arts and skill tree.
- Beasts that come for you or leave you be, elites and champions, finds common to rare, gear with critical hits, blocks and mana, bounties and a bestiary.
- The site's own address: fight.nornloom.com.
