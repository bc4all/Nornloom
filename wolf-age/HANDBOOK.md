## Fighting

There is no fight screen. The world runs round you in real time: beasts wander, notice you, chase you and give up when you lead them far from home. Calm beasts leave you be until you strike them; fierce ones come for you. You fight where you stand, and other beasts nearby may join in.

### The buttons

On a phone the fight buttons sit in the bottom right corner. Strike is the big one in the corner; your two arts sit to its left, the dodge roll straight above it, and your two spells on the ring beyond. The draughts sit at the top right under the menu, each with the number you carry.

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Strike: J, Space or a click | a blow with your weapon; hold it to charge a heavier one. A bow or a staff shoots from afar |
| Tap a beast | make it your target, with a gold ring: your blows and shots go at it, even as you walk away, so a bow or a staff keeps shooting it while you back off. You still walk and strike yourself |
| Arts: U and I, or 1 and 2 | your class's two arts. Each costs mana and is ready again after its wait |
| Spells: Q and E, or 3 and 4 | the two spells in your slots, learnt in the skill tree; they cost more mana |
| Dodge: K or Shift | a quick roll the way you walk; nothing can touch you for most of it, so roll through a beast's blow as it lands |
| Draughts: H and M, or 5 and 6 | drink a healing or a mana draught on the spot |
| Pause: P | stop the world while you think |

A button that waits says so plainly: a dark slice sweeps round it like a clock hand, a bright arc fills its rim and the seconds left stand large in the middle. A button short of mana turns blue-grey, with its price in red on the rim.

### Mana

Arts and spells cost mana. In a fight it comes back slowly, with a little more for every blow you land, so a hero who throws every art runs dry and wants a mana draught. From level 30 the prices grow as your mana does, so an art always takes about the same bite.

### Rest and draughts

Once no beast has been after you for 5 seconds, and you have struck no blow, you rest where you stand: health comes back from empty to full in 3 minutes, and mana in 2. A draught is quicker, and Ulf, Groa and every healer rest you to full at once for 5 gold a level.

### Beasts

A beast's name is coloured by how hard the fight is: green is easy, yellow fair, light red hard, dark red deadly. Watch its wind-up and the mark it puts on the ground, step or roll out of it, and strike while it recovers. Elites and champions are tougher and carry traits; the FAQ says what each trait does.

Strongboxes stand guarded: open one and its guardian rises. Bring it down for the gold, gear or artifact inside.

### A fall

Losing all your health ends the fight. You wake in the nearest village whose waystone you have touched, or at home if that is nearer, with 25 percent of your health. A fall costs 10 percent of the gold you carry, and one time in 10 a piece of the gear you wear breaks and is gone.

## The skill trees

Levels bring skill points: one a level up to level 20, then fewer as you climb, 53 by level 99. Spend them on the Skills screen. Your class has two trees of its own, its craft with its two arts and its way of staying alive, and every class shares the Nine Worlds: fire, storm and frost.

Every skill shows from the start, with the level it opens at and lines from the skills it needs first. Tap one to read it and learn it. Higher ranks cost more points and wait for higher levels, and the last row of a tree wants 15 points spent in that tree. No hero fills every tree, so choose. A reset gives every point back for 25 gold a level, and for the same gold you can take up another class.

Fire leaves a beast burning, frost freezes it where it stands, and storm strikes all round. Ashen Blows sets beasts burning with your critical hits, Mjölnir's Echo calls lightning down round the beast you crit, and Deep Cold makes frozen beasts take more from you.



Each table lists a tree's skills from the top row down. "Opens at" is the level of the first rank; higher ranks wait longer and cost more (see [The numbers](#the-numbers)). A value in brackets is what the top rank gives.

## Gear and armour sets

Gear goes in six slots: head, body, legs, weapon, shield and amulet. It adds health, attack, defence and mana, and better pieces add critical hits, blocks, gold or experience. Finds come common, uncommon or rare, and named treasures carry a power of their own. Smiths sell gear, with a weapon and a charm for every class, and healers sell draughts. Beasts drop gear for every class alike.

Each class has its weapons: swords, axes, spears, hammers and shields for the Berserker and the Shieldmaiden, bows for the Huntress, staffs for the Seidr-worker, wands for the Lifeweaver. Bows, staffs, wands and great axes take both hands. A Berserker may hold a second one-handed weapon in the off hand instead of a shield; it gives a quarter of its stats.

Every class has armour sets made for it, the first from level 8 and the last from level 85. Two pieces of a set worn add a bonus, all four add another on top. Beasts of a set's levels now and then leave a piece, most often of your own class's set; the Backpack shows how far each set you wear is complete.

Gear comes in tiers, each from a level: tier 1 from level 1, tier 2 from level 8, tier 3 from level 16, tier 4 from level 26, tier 5 from level 38, tier 6 from level 52, tier 7 from level 68, tier 8 from level 85.

### The forge

At a smith choose Forge. Break gear you no longer want down into scrap iron, rune shards and dwarf embers, then spend them with a little gold to hone a piece up to +5, or to give a piece a new quality. Ore you mine is smelted into iron there too.

### Bag and chest

The bag holds 40 pieces of gear; draughts take no room. Your chest at home holds 200 more and is safe when you fall. Near home, the Bag's Chest tab puts gear away and sets the loot filter: which finds you keep, and which are broken down for the forge where they drop. Rare finds are always kept.

## Gathering and brewing

Herbs, ore veins and fishing spots stand in the wild. Walk up to one and press Gather; it grows back in 4 minutes. Herb lore, Mining, Fishing and Brewing are crafts that grow with use up to level 10, and the richer finds of the far lands want a practised hand. Brew healing and mana draughts on the Bag's Crafts tab.

Each craft is a tree on the Bag's Crafts tab, like the skill trees: the finds down the middle, the smith's tools to one side and the craft's steps to the other, meeting in the master's step at level 10. Steps open by themselves as the craft grows: the richer finds, quicker regrowth, fuller yields, a rune shard now and then from a vein, and draughts brewed two at once. In Brewing the healing and mana draughts branch apart; tap one to brew it.

What you gather is in the Bag, on the All and Goods tabs. Any trader buys it, a little gold a piece. Online it can also go on the market, where heroes of the same kind of world, peaceful or PvP, can buy it.

Any smith also makes gathering tools from your goods: a sickle, a pick and a net, each in three grades, each made after the one below. A tool takes no room and helps every pick of its craft. Smiths tie gathering knots too, worn in the amulet slot, which help every pick of every kind. Both bring a second find more often and more craft XP a pick, but a master's third find stays rare.

Brewing also makes boon draughts, and Cooking, a fifth craft, turns fish and herbs into meals. A draught or a meal makes you stronger for a while: blows land true or glance off more often, skills come ready sooner, mana comes back faster. One draught and one meal can work at once, and a new one takes the place of the last of its kind. Cook and brew from the Bag's Crafts tab.

The richer finds want more of the craft: tier 1 from level 1, tier 2 from level 3, tier 3 from level 6, out of 10.

## Bounties

The Quests tab offers 3 bounties a day and one a week, fitted to your level: slay so many of a beast, bring down elites or champions, or open strongboxes. They pay experience and gold, and the weekly one a rare find. New ones come each day and each week.

## After the story

Once Ragnarök is told, the world can be reborn. Speak to Skuld at the well and choose how the beasts of every land stand against you; she lets you choose again later.

| Choice | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| The world as it was | Every land keeps the beasts it always had. |
| The world reborn | The beasts of every land rise to your level, and leave the finds of your day. |
| Reborn in iron | They rise to your level, tougher and harder of bite. They leave half as much gold again, and more finds. |
| Reborn in fire | They rise to your level, far tougher and far harder of bite. They leave more than twice the gold, and far more finds. |

### The Norns' descent

From level 99 a way down opens at Skuld's well. Every floor is a deep cave carved afresh each day, tougher and better paid than the floor above. Find the hole to go down. Leave a floor within 4 minutes and it is a swift one: each swift floor in a row adds 10% to the pay of the floors after it, up to 10 in a row, and a slow floor breaks the run. Every 5th floor keeps a gilded hoard. The ladder of any floor climbs back to the well.

### Renown

Past level 99 the experience bar keeps filling, and each fill is a point of renown. Spend it on small gifts, 50 ranks in all; past that, renown is a number to be proud of.
