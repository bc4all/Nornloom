# The Skald's Road

*A walkthrough of every saga in Nornloom, step by step.*

Groa reads the Norns' threads and they are fraying: the old stories are being forgotten, and every story forgotten brings the Wolf's day nearer. You are a skald with a memory that keeps everything. Walk into the stories, live them at the gods' side, and bring them back to be told.

This book walks the same road. Every step says where to go, who is waiting there and what they say, what to fight, and what you are paid at the end. The skald in these pages is called Sigrun; in the game every line says your own hero's name.

How to read a step:

- Where tells you the land (its name shows under your hero's name in the top corner) and a bearing from the step before, counted in steps on the map. The line in italics is the hint the game itself gives in the quest log.
- Walk into someone to talk to them. People with something for you have a mark over their heads. Foes of the sagas only stand in the world while their part of the story is playing.
- Every riddle has its answer folded away under it. A wrong answer costs nothing; you can try again.
- Level is the level the quest is offered at. The lands further out are locked until you are strong enough to walk into them.
- The waystone in every land carries you home to Eikthorp for a small toll, which is why the way to a new saga is counted from Eikthorp.

Every name, line, level and reward below comes from the game's own data. The book is regenerated when the game changes, so it follows the version that is live.

## Prologue

### The Waking

*Level 1 · given by Groa the völva in Eikthorp · 4 steps*

You wake on Ulf's bench in Eikthorp with a verse still in your mouth. Nothing here is a fight yet. Four people, four conversations, from one end of the village to the other, and at the end of it an old woman with thread in her lap who has been waiting all night.

**1. Wake at Ulf's fire**

Where: Ulf the skald, in Eikthorp. About 30 steps south-east of Groa the völva. *Ulf is at his fire by the mead tent, at the south end of the village.*

![](images/quests/prologue-waking-00.png)

> 'You are awake, Sigrun. You were saying verses in your sleep again, the whole of Bjorn's grandfather's drowning-song, and you have heard it once. Once, at a funeral, when you were nine.' He turns the spit. 'I have been a skald forty years and I could not do that. It is not a gift, exactly. Gifts get used up. Go and stretch your legs before Astrid packs the good stock away.'

Then press "Stand up".

**2. Take a deck from Astrid at the trading post**

Where: Astrid the trader, in Eikthorp. About 40 steps north of Ulf the skald. *Astrid's post is at the north end of the village, by the well.*

![](images/quests/prologue-waking-01.png)

> 'You look like a person with nothing to trade, Sigrun. Here.' She slides a bundle across the plank. 'A deck. Every fighter from here to the fens settles things with a hand of Bindrune, because the alternative is settling them with axes and I sell to both sides. Learn them, and come back when you own something I want.'

Then press "Take the deck".

**3. Look at the well behind Astrid's post**

Where: Skuld, a Norn, in Eikthorp. About 12 steps south-east of Astrid the trader. *The well is behind Astrid's post, on the east side of the village. Someone is standing at it.*

![](images/quests/prologue-waking-02.png)

> A woman stands at the well with her back to you. She does not turn around. 'Not yet,' she says. 'Go and see the old woman first. She has been awake all night for reasons she will explain badly.'

Then press "Go to Groa".

**4. Sit with Groa at her hut**

Where: Groa the völva, in Eikthorp. About 40 steps west of Skuld, a Norn. *Groa's hut is at the west end of the village, by the west lane.*

![](images/quests/prologue-waking-03.png)

> Groa has thread wound round both hands and is not looking at it. 'Sit down. No, there. I have been pulling at this since the middle of the night and it keeps coming apart in the same place, and the same place is now.' She drops it in her lap. 'My husband went north with a giant-slayer eleven years ago and I have read every thread in this village since, so you will believe me when I say I know what a fraying looks like.'

Then press "Listen".

> 'Take these for the road, Sigrun; you will want them. Now sit still, because the next part is the part that matters.'

Reward: 20 gold, 10 XP, Healing draught.

*The Norns live at a well under a root of Yggdrasil and water the tree from it. The fraying threads, the skald and Eikthorp are the game's invention. Source: Poetic Edda, Völuspá st. 19–20; Prose Edda, Gylfaginning ch. 15–16.*

## Act 0: The Hanged God

Levels 1 to 4, Eikthorp and Ashgrove Meadows. Home, a well, a pond, and the first of the stories.

### The Hanged God

*Level 1 · given by Groa the völva in Eikthorp · 7 steps*

The first story is the oldest one Odin tells about himself. A Norn at the well sends you east into the meadows, where an old man with one eye is looking for a tree to hang from. Keep the beasts off him for nine nights and he comes down with the runes.

**Taking it up.** Find Groa the völva in Eikthorp.

![](images/quests/hanged-god-00.png)

> Sit, child. I read the threads the Norns spin, and they are fraying: the old stories are being forgotten, and each one forgotten brings the Wolf's day nearer. Ulf says you have a skald's memory. Go to the well behind Astrid's post. Someone is waiting to tell you why.

**1. Speak with Skuld at the well behind the trading post**

Where: Skuld, a Norn, in Eikthorp. About 40 steps east of Groa the völva. *Skuld waits by the well behind Astrid's post. Norns do not wait forever.*

![](images/quests/hanged-god-01.png)

> I am Skuld, youngest of the Norns. My sisters and I water the ash Yggdrasil from this well, and we weave what has been, what is, and what shall be. But threads are only threads: a story no one remembers unravels. Walk into the old stories, Sigrun, live them at the gods' side, and bring them back to be told. Begin with the one-eyed wanderer in the meadows. He is older than he looks.

Then press "I will remember".

**2. Answer the Wanderer's riddle in Ashgrove Meadows**

Where: The Wanderer, in Ashgrove Meadows. About 30 steps east of Skuld, a Norn. *The Wanderer stands in the meadows east of the gate. Answer him well.*

![](images/quests/hanged-god-02.png)

> The old man leans on his staff and peers at you from under a wide hat. 'A skald, are you? Then answer me as the giant Vafþrúðnir once had to. What is the name of the horse that draws the day over mankind?'

- Skinfaxi, Shining-mane
- Sleipnir, the eight-legged
- Hrímfaxi, Frost-mane

<details><summary>The answer</summary>

Skinfaxi, Shining-mane.

> 'Skinfaxi. His mane lights the sky. Hrímfaxi draws the night, and the froth from his bit is the dew.' The old man's one eye glitters. 'Good. Now walk with me to the pond. Something there wants to talk to you.'

</details>

**3. Speak with Mímir's head at the meadow pond**

Where: Mímir's head, in Ashgrove Meadows. About 40 steps south-east of The Wanderer. *Mímir's head lies in the reeds by the meadow pond, south of Bjorn's farm.*

![](images/quests/hanged-god-03.png)

> A head, and nothing else, rests among the reeds; the water around it is clear as glass. 'I am Mímir. The Vanir cut me off, and Odin keeps me with herbs and asks me things. He came here once to drink from my well, and I asked an eye for the drink. He paid it. Look at him. He would pay it again.' The Wanderer, behind you, says nothing.

Then press "Look at the Wanderer".

**4. Return to the Wanderer**

Where: The Wanderer, in Ashgrove Meadows. About 40 steps north-west of Mímir's head. *The Wanderer has gone back to the grove in the meadows. He has a rope.*

![](images/quests/hanged-god-04.png)

> 'I hang tonight,' the Wanderer says, and climbs the ash by the grove with a rope and a spear. 'Nine nights, wounded with the spear, given to myself, no bread, no horn. Do not cut me down, whatever I say. Keep the beasts off the tree.'

Then press "Keep watch".

**5. Keep the night watch: drive off 3 creatures in the meadows**

Where: Ashgrove Meadows. Right beside The Wanderer. *The Wanderer hangs in the tree. Keep the creatures of the meadows away from him: three of them, at least.*

![](images/quests/hanged-god-05.png)

Win 3 fights against anything that roams Ashgrove Meadows.

**6. Cut the Wanderer down on the ninth night**

Where: The Wanderer, in Ashgrove Meadows. Right beside where you fought. *Nine nights are up. Go back to the tree.*

![](images/quests/hanged-god-06.png)

> On the ninth night the Wanderer screams and drops from the tree, and in his fist are runes. 'I took them up, and fell back. Fehu, Uruz, Thurisaz: wealth, strength, the thurse's thorn. Three for you, skald.' He straightens, and the hat comes off, and the one eye is Odin's. 'Tell Groa the first thread is whole.'

Then press "Take the runes".

**7. Retell the story to Groa**

Where: Groa the völva, in Eikthorp. About 70 steps west of The Wanderer. *Tell me the story, so it is remembered.*

![](images/quests/hanged-god-07.png)

> Groa stirs the fire. 'Tell it back to me, skald, so it is remembered. How many nights did Odin hang on the tree?'

- Nine
- Three
- Twelve

<details><summary>The answer</summary>

Nine.

> 'Nine nights, wounded with a spear, himself to himself. So Hávamál says. Well told.'

</details>

> The first thread holds, Sigrun. Keep the runes close; the Wanderer will find you again where the stories fray. He always does.

Reward: 86 gold, 118 XP, the card Mímir's Head, Talisman of three runes.

*Odin hangs nine nights on the windy tree, spear-wounded, 'given to Odin, myself to myself', and takes up the runes. His eye lies in Mímir's well. The skald and the fraying threads are the game's invention. Source: Poetic Edda, Hávamál st. 138–141; Völuspá st. 28; Prose Edda, Gylfaginning ch. 15.*

## Act 1: The Young World

Levels 5 to 31. The gods are young, the treasures are new, and every trouble has Loki somewhere in it. The road leaves the hand-built lands south and east, then north into the dwarves' fields.

### The Builder's Price

*Level 5 · given by Odin the Wanderer in Barrow Hills · after The Hanged God · 5 steps*

The gods hired a stranger to wall Asgard in a single winter and promised him things that were not theirs to give. The wall is almost done. Somewhere in the Barrow Hills a stallion is doing most of the work, and Loki has an idea about that.

**Taking it up.** Find Odin the Wanderer in Barrow Hills, about 50 steps south of Eikthorp.

![](images/quests/builders-price-00.png)

> The gods hired a builder to raise a wall around Asgard in one winter. His price: Freyja, and the sun and the moon. We agreed, on Loki's advice, because no one can build a wall in a winter. Look at the old stones down there. It is nearly done. Go and see how.

**1. Watch the Builder at work by the old wall**

Where: The Builder, in Barrow Hills. About 20 steps south of Odin the Wanderer. *The Builder works at the ruined wall in the south of the hills. Do not get in his way.*

![](images/quests/builders-price-01.png)

> The builder is broad as a door and never stops moving. 'Three days to summer, and only the gate left. Freyja is mine, and the sun, and the moon. Svaðilfari! More stone!' Out in the dark a stallion drags a boulder the size of a house.

Then press "Watch the stallion".

**2. Tell Odin what is building the wall**

Where: Odin the Wanderer, in Barrow Hills. About 20 steps north of The Builder. *Come back and tell me what you saw at the wall.*

![](images/quests/builders-price-02.png)

> Odin listens. 'A wall in a winter is impossible. So what is doing the impossible?'

- Dwarves dig for him at night
- He is simply the strongest man alive
- His stallion Svaðilfari hauls the stone

<details><summary>The answer</summary>

His stallion Svaðilfari hauls the stone.

> 'The horse. The gods let him use it, on Loki's word. So Loki will lose him the horse, or lose his head. He is hiding somewhere in these hills. Find him.'

</details>

**3. Find Loki hiding in the hills**

Where: Loki, a sly stranger, in Barrow Hills. About 40 steps south of Odin the Wanderer. *Loki hides somewhere among the mounds in the south-west. He is good at it.*

![](images/quests/builders-price-03.png)

> You find Loki behind a grave-mound, very still. 'The gods have promised me a bad death if the builder wins. Fine. Tonight, when Svaðilfari goes for stone, a mare will call from the wood, and no stallion alive stays at work for that. Keep the builder busy. Do not ask what the mare is.'

Then press "Do not ask".

**4. Face the Builder when his stallion is gone**

Where: The Builder, unmasked, level 8, in Barrow Hills. About 30 steps east of Loki, a sly stranger. *The mare has drawn Svaðilfari into the wood. The builder has no horse and no wall, and a giant's temper. Face him by the ruins; Thor is on his way from the east.*

![](images/quests/builders-price-04.png)

A fight of Bindrune against The Builder, unmasked, level 8. This foe is only here while this step of the story is playing. Walk into it when you are ready.

**5. Retell the story to Odin**

Where: Odin the Wanderer, in Barrow Hills. About 40 steps north of The Builder, unmasked. *Tell it back to me, skald.*

![](images/quests/builders-price-05.png)

> 'The builder was a rock-giant in disguise, and Thor's hammer paid his wages. Tell me the rest, skald: some months later, what did Loki bring to Asgard?'

- A grey foal with eight legs, Sleipnir
- The builder's severed head
- A new wall, finished

<details><summary>The answer</summary>

A grey foal with eight legs, Sleipnir.

> 'Sleipnir, best of horses, my own. Loki was the mare. The gods broke an oath that winter, and Völuspá does not forgive it.'

</details>

> Sleipnir carries me now over sea and sky. Take his likeness, and remember: the gods kept the wall and lost their honour.

Reward: 133 gold, 470 XP, the card Sleipnir.

*Oaths mattered enormously to the Norse. The gods break the spirit of theirs here, and Völuspá treats it as a stain: 'who had mixed the air with venom?' Source: Prose Edda, Gylfaginning ch. 42; Völuspá st. 25–26.*

### The Mead of Poetry

*Level 8 · given by Ulf the skald in Eikthorp · after The Builder's Price · 8 steps*

Kvasir, who could answer anything, went to dinner with two dwarves and did not come back. Follow his trail from the fens to a giant's farm and into a mountain, where the mead made from him is kept, and watch Odin steal it.

**Taking it up.** Find Ulf the skald in Eikthorp.

![](images/quests/mead-of-poetry-00.png)

> Kvasir the wise sat at my fire last week; there is no question that man cannot answer. Then two dwarves, Fjalar and Galar, invited him to their hall, and he has not been seen since. They are in the fens. Go and ask them, and be careful what you drink.

**1. Question Fjalar the dwarf in the fens**

Where: Fjalar the dwarf, in Jotun Fens. About 50 steps south-east of Ulf the skald. *Fjalar and Galar keep a hall in the western fens. Ask them what became of Kvasir.*

![](images/quests/mead-of-poetry-01.png)

> 'Kvasir?' says the dwarf, too quickly. 'Choked. On his own wisdom, would you believe it; no one could ask him enough questions to let it out.' Behind him two vats stand covered, and the cloth is dark at the bottom.

Then press "Look at the vats".

**2. Learn where the mead went**

Where: Suttungr the giant, in Jotun Fens. About 30 steps north-east of Fjalar the dwarf. *Suttungr the giant took the mead from the dwarves. He is the largest thing in the fens.*

![](images/quests/mead-of-poetry-02.png)

> A giant fills the path. 'I am Suttungr. Those dwarves drowned my father Gillingr and dropped a millstone on my mother, so I rowed them out to a skerry and let them buy their lives. With mead. Brewed from a wise man's blood and honey, they say. It sits in Hnitbjörg now, and my daughter Gunnlöð sits on it. No one drinks.'

Then press "So that is Kvasir".

**3. Meet Bölverk at Baugi's farm**

Where: Bölverk, a farmhand, in Jotun Fens. About 20 steps east of Suttungr the giant. *A one-eyed farmhand called Bölverk works for the giant Baugi in the eastern fens. You know that eye.*

![](images/quests/mead-of-poetry-03.png)

> An old farmhand leans on a scythe at the edge of Baugi's fields. One eye. 'Call me Bölverk, evil-worker. Baugi is Suttungr's brother; his nine slaves cut each other's throats over my whetstone this morning, so he needs hands for the summer. Help me earn a drink of his brother's mead: the trolls are in the barley.'

Then press "Take up the scythe".

**4. Work Bölverk's summer: clear 3 trolls from Baugi's fields**

Where: Jotun Fens. About 16 steps south-east of Bölverk, a farmhand. *The summer's work is trolls in the barley. Three should satisfy Baugi.*

![](images/quests/mead-of-poetry-04.png)

Win 3 fights against fen trolls.

Fen trolls roam here; walk into one to fight it.

**5. Collect Bölverk's wages**

Where: Bölverk, a farmhand, in Jotun Fens. About 16 steps north-west of where you fought. *The summer is done. Bölverk wants his drink.*

![](images/quests/mead-of-poetry-05.png)

> 'Baugi owes me a drink and cannot pay: Suttungr said no. So Baugi bores into the mountain with the auger Rati, and cheats: I blew into the hole and the chips flew back at me. He bored again. I go in as a snake. Wait for me inside Hnitbjörg, the cave under Ironwood, and be ready to run.'

Then press "Go to Hnitbjörg".

**6. Enter Hnitbjörg, the cave under Ironwood, and find Gunnlöð**

Where: Gunnlöð, in Hnitbjorg, the cave in Ironwood. Go down into the cave. Its door is about 50 steps north-east of Bölverk, a farmhand. *Hnitbjörg is the cave under Ironwood. Gunnlöð keeps the mead inside.*

![](images/quests/mead-of-poetry-06.png)

> A woman sits in the dark with three empty vessels, Óðrerir, Boðn and Són. 'Three nights he lay with me and I promised him three drinks. He drank each vessel dry in one draught, and flew out an eagle. My father is following him. In eagle shape.'

Then press "Run".

**7. Hold off Suttungr in eagle shape while Odin reaches Asgard**

Where: Suttungr in eagle shape, level 11, in Ironwood. About 7 steps east of Gunnlöð. *Suttungr chases Odin as an eagle over the east of Ironwood. Bring the eagle down before it reaches him.*

![](images/quests/mead-of-poetry-07.png)

A fight of Bindrune against Suttungr in eagle shape, level 11. This foe is only here while this step of the story is playing. Walk into it when you are ready.

**8. Retell the story to Ulf**

Where: Ulf the skald, in Eikthorp. About 130 steps west of Suttungr in eagle shape. *Sit. Tell it.*

![](images/quests/mead-of-poetry-08.png)

> Ulf pours. 'The gods set out vats and Odin spat the mead into them; what he let fall behind is the rhymester's share, and every bad poet has drunk it. Tell me: what was the mead brewed from?'

- Kvasir's blood and honey
- Iðunn's apples and rain
- The tears of Gunnlöð

<details><summary>The answer</summary>

Kvasir's blood and honey.

> 'Blood and honey. That is why poetry is called Kvasir's blood. Well told; you may have a real drink now.'

</details>

> Kvasir lives on in every good verse. A drop of the mead for you, then, and the story stays told.

Reward: 239 gold, 1324 XP, the card Kvasir the Wise, Draught of Óðrerir.

*Poetry was a divine gift. The kennings 'Kvasir's blood' and 'Óðrerir's sea' for poetry are real, and the rhymester's share explains bad verse. The vial is the game's invention. Source: Prose Edda, Skáldskaparmál ch. 1 (G57); Hávamál st. 104–110.*

### The Dwarves' Wager

*Level 12 · given by Thor in Nidavellir · after The Mead of Poetry · 7 steps*

Loki cut off Sif's hair for a joke and has to replace it. He goes to the dwarves, bets his head that they cannot outdo their rivals, and then cheats. Most of the gods' treasures come out of this one afternoon at the forge.

**Taking it up.** Find Thor in Nidavellir, about 180 steps east of Eikthorp.

![](images/quests/dwarves-wager-00.png)

> Loki cut off my wife's hair in her sleep. All of it. I have promised to break every bone in his body unless the dwarves make her hair of gold, and he has run off to see about it. Go after him. If the dwarves do the work, well; if not, bring me his bones.

**1. Speak with Sif**

Where: Sif, in Nidavellir. About 8 steps north of Thor. *Sif is in the hamlet, hiding her head. Speak with her, then find Loki.*

![](images/quests/dwarves-wager-01.png)

> Sif wears a scarf over her head and her eyes are red. 'Gold that grows like hair, Loki promised. Dwarf-work. If it does not, Thor will kill him, and I will let him.'

Then press "Find Loki".

**2. Find Loki at the forges of Niðavellir**

Where: Loki, in Nidavellir. About 20 steps south-east of Sif. *Loki is somewhere near the dwarves' forges, talking too much.*

![](images/quests/dwarves-wager-02.png)

> Loki is watching the forges. 'The Sons of Ívaldi have already made the hair, a ship that folds into a pocket, and a spear that never misses. But now I have bet my head with Brokkr that his brother Eitri cannot make three things as good. Stand witness, would you? Someone should.'

Then press "Witness the wager".

**3. Stand witness to the wager**

Where: Brokkr the dwarf, in Nidavellir. About 11 steps north of Loki. *Brokkr is at the bellows and Eitri at the fire. The wager needs a witness.*

![](images/quests/dwarves-wager-03.png)

> 'Witness this,' Brokkr says. 'Loki's head against three treasures. Eitri works the fire; I work the bellows and must not stop, not for anything. Keep that fly away from me.' A fat fly is already circling the smithy.

Then press "Swear as witness".

**4. Keep the fly off Brokkr while the treasures are forged**

Where: A fat, biting fly, level 15, in Nidavellir. About 4 steps north of Brokkr the dwarf. *The fly bit Eitri's hand as he pulled out the boar, then Brokkr's neck as he took out the ring. Catch it, north of the forge, before the hammer is done.*

![](images/quests/dwarves-wager-04.png)

A fight of Bindrune against A fat, biting fly, level 15. This foe is only here while this step of the story is playing. Walk into it when you are ready.

**5. Speak with Eitri about the hammer**

Where: Eitri the smith, in Nidavellir. Right beside a fat, biting fly. *Eitri has the last treasure out of the fire. See what the fly did to it.*

![](images/quests/dwarves-wager-05.png)

> Eitri lifts a hammer from the coals. 'Gullinbursti, the boar with golden bristles. Draupnir, the ring that drips eight rings every ninth night. And this. The fly bit Brokkr's eyelid as I drew it out, and the bellows stopped for one breath. The handle is short. It is still the best thing I have ever made.' That is how the story goes; the flaw is part of it.

Then press "Carry the treasures to the gods".

**6. Judge Loki's loophole**

Where: Loki, in Nidavellir. About 16 steps south of Eitri the smith. *Brokkr wants Loki's head. Loki has a point about his neck. You are the witness.*

![](images/quests/dwarves-wager-06.png)

> The gods judged Mjölnir the best of the treasures, and Brokkr came for Loki's head. Loki ran in shoes that run over sky and sea; Thor caught him. Now Loki grins at the dwarf's knife. 'Take my head. It is yours. But not one finger's breadth of my neck; the neck was not in the bet.' Brokkr looks at you, the witness.

- The head cannot come off without the neck; let him cut
- Let Loki keep both and pay in gold
- The bet was for the head only; the neck is not his

<details><summary>The answer</summary>

The bet was for the head only; the neck is not his.

> Brokkr snarls, but a witness is a witness. He takes an awl and a thong called Vartari and sews Loki's lips shut instead. Loki tears them open later. He always does.

</details>

**7. Retell the story to Thor**

Where: Thor, in Nidavellir. About 20 steps west of Loki. *Tell me why my hammer's handle is short.*

![](images/quests/dwarves-wager-07.png)

> Thor weighs the hammer. 'It comes back to my hand when I throw it, and the handle is a hair too short. Tell me why, skald.'

- Eitri ran out of iron
- Loki filed it down out of spite
- A fly bit Brokkr's eyelid and the bellows stopped a moment

<details><summary>The answer</summary>

A fly bit Brokkr's eyelid and the bellows stopped a moment.

> 'The fly was Loki, of course. He gave us Mjölnir trying to spoil it. That is Loki.'

## The side sagas

Five stories off the main road. Each opens once the main saga it hangs from is done (the rune stones from the very start), and each pays a god's card.

*Level 1 · given by Ulf the skald in Eikthorp · 5 steps*

**Taking it up.** Find Ulf the skald in Eikthorp.
