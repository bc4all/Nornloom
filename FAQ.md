# Nornloom: questions players ask

The same page as the FAQ in the game's help book (Help, then FAQ), kept here so it can be read and linked without opening the game. The numbers come from the game's own rules, so when a rule changes this page changes with the next release. A question that is not here fits a [blank issue](https://github.com/bc4all/Nornloom/issues/new).

## The story game

**Does Nornloom cost anything?**

No. It is free to play, alone or online, with no adverts and nothing to buy. Playing alone needs no account and no sign-in.

**Where is my hero saved?**

A solo hero is saved on the device you play on, in the browser's storage or the app's data, and nothing about it is sent anywhere until you sign in. Signed in, a copy is kept for your account on the worlds' server, so the hero follows you to another device and comes back after that storage is cleared; the later of two devices' saves wins. The title screen keeps every hero on the device, so you can have several. A hero on a world lives on the world's server and follows your sign-in the same way.

**Can I change how a myth ends?**

No. Every main saga is a real myth from the Eddas and keeps its real ending: Thor loses to Elli, Baldr dies. You choose how the story gets there, and the retelling at the end asks what happened. Where the game invents something, the quest log says so.

**How long is the story?**

Thirteen main sagas in one chain, from Odin on the tree at level 1 to Ragnarök at level 80, and five side sagas along the way. The map climbs to level 99, so the last lands are there for the hunting once the story is told.

**I answered the retelling wrong. Is the saga lost?**

No. A wrong answer costs nothing; answer again. The reward waits for the right one.

**What should I do next?**

The HUD names the next thing to do and the Bag keeps the quest log. In the world, a ! over a villager is a quest to offer and a bright ? means they have something for you now. When nothing is offered, the beasts and the strongboxes around you are worth gold, XP and cards until the next rune stone opens.

**A rune stone will not let me through.**

The banner at the border says the level you need. Level up on the beasts and quests where you are, and make your deck the best it can be: traders sell cards, the smiths temper them, and Hrafn's yard lets you try a deck against anything with nothing at stake. The Map button on the world screen shows every land with the levels of its beasts, misted where you cannot go yet.

**I lost a card in a fight. How do I get cards back?**

A defeat takes one random card from your deck, never below the 20 it needs to be played. A win pays a card from the foe's people, quests pay cards, and the traders sell them. Running from a fight you picked costs nothing at all.

**Why does my Warrior not heal between turns?**

Armor and Vigor lost in Bindrune are lost for the rest of the hand. That is the game's design: a fight costs something even when you win. Wards take the hits meant for your Thread, a Mend of Glory buys Thread back, and the smiths temper Warriors and Wards so they last longer.

**The beasts are too strong. Is there an easier setting?**

There is no difficulty setting; the world is the setting. A name in green is an easy fight and yellow a fair one; leave the red ones for later. Rest with Ulf or Groa before a hard fight, wear the best gear you can afford, and spar with Hrafn to learn a deck before you bet on it. A beast two or more levels below you keeps its distance.

**Can I play in my own language?**

The title screen offers seven tongues, and the game follows your device's language until you choose one. Card text still speaks English.

**Does it work on a phone?**

Yes. In the browser, hold the d-pad in the corner to walk and tap a card to read it. On Android there is also an app on Google Play, the same game with the same worlds; signing in there opens the site in your browser for a moment and comes back.

**Will my hero survive an update?**

Usually. When the map changes, a hero from the older world wakes at home in Eikthorp with everything it had. While the game is young a save may still be reset between versions, and the About page says so.

**Where do I report a bug or ask for something?**

On GitHub: the title screen and the About page have a button for it. Bugs, ideas and questions all go there, and they are read.

## Playing online

**What do I need to play online?**

A sign-in from the Play online screen and an age of 16 or more. Playing online is free. A hero on a world belongs to your sign-in and follows it between devices; if you enter from a second device, the first is let go.

**Can I take my solo hero online?**

No. A hero on a world is made on that world and starts at level 1; a solo hero stays a solo hero, though it follows your sign-in between devices. The worlds share the map and the sagas, so the story is the same. You may keep up to three heroes on each world.

**What is the difference between Midgard and Jotunheim?**

The same map, its own people, its own rules. On Midgard a duel needs both to agree and nothing is at stake. On Jotunheim, outside the villages, a hero at most 10 levels above you may challenge you without asking: you have a moment to stand or flee, and the loser hands over 10% of the gold they carry. Cards and gear never change hands in a duel.

**How do I find a friend and play with them?**

Enter the same world. The board lists who is on, and /who in the chat does too. Walk up to them, tap their name in Nearby to whisper, or write /w Name and your words. /duel Name asks for a hand of Bindrune; /trade Name, standing within 2 tiles, opens a table to trade at.

**Can I give cards, gear or gold to another hero?**

Yes, in three ways. Face to face: /trade Name, both lay goods on the table and both must accept. By parcel: any trader who keeps parcels sends gold, items and cards to a hero by name for a small fee, and returns it after 30 days unclaimed. On the market: at the same traders, cards and items are put up for gold, and the world takes 3% of a sale. Never for real money; the Terms forbid it.

**Someone is bothering me. What can I do?**

Write /block Name in the chat, or press Block at the head of their private thread: their words, whispers, trade and duel offers no longer reach you. For harassment, threats or a foul name, write to privacy@nornloom.com with the hero's name; the keepers can mute, rename or ban. On Jotunheim a forced challenge on open ground still comes, block or not; Midgard has none.

**Why is a hero's name red?**

They are marked: three forced wins within an hour on Jotunheim. For the next hour they may be challenged anywhere, by anyone, safe ground and level gap set aside.

**Why was my hero's name refused?**

A name has 3 to 20 letters, is unique across the worlds (look-alike spellings count as the same), carries no offensive word in any language, is not a name from the sagas, and does not pretend to be a keeper. A name that slips through can be locked later, and the hero waits until you give it a new one.

**The world let me go. What happened?**

Ten minutes without doing anything, and the world closes the door; it warns you a minute ahead. Nothing is lost. Go back to the Worlds screen and enter again. A hero at a table is never idle.

**My connection dropped in a fight.**

Against a beast the world waits two minutes for you; come back and the fight goes on where it stood. Longer than that counts as running: the beast's bite and the XP the win would have paid. In a duel the grace is one minute, then the fight is forfeit.

**Who can read the chat?**

Nearby reaches the heroes around you; a whisper reaches one hero. Nothing said is stored, so nobody reads it later. Words the name rules refuse are dotted out as they pass.

**What does the world keep about me, and how do I delete it?**

The Privacy page of the game's help book lists it: your sign-in's id, your heroes and a ledger of their gold. Delete my account on the title screen removes it all after 7 days; signing in again within those days keeps it.
